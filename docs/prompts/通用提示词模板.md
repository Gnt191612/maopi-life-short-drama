# 通用提示词模板（任何「白模 + AI 写实化」短剧都能直接套用）

> 只收和具体剧情无关、换一部片照样能用的句式。`{花括号}` 处换成自己的内容。代码版在 `cloud/h3_prompt.py`。

## 1. H3 视频提示词骨架（首帧模式 I2VA）

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, {景别}. The people shown in <Picture 1> ({每人一句身份：a 20-year-old Chinese male college student, ...}) remain in the positions and framing established by <Picture 1>, preserving their faces, hairstyles, clothing, body shapes, the room layout, props and lighting. {这一镜头的动作：首帧状态 → 动作开始 → 连续发展 → 结果或反应}. {运镜句}, matching the camera movement of the scene. Everyone moves with natural human timing: small breathing movements, occasional blinks, subtle weight shifts and relaxed hands; clothing folds and hair move naturally with each motion. Setting: {场景一句}. {说话句 或 不说话句} Natural skin texture, true-to-life colors, soft realistic lighting consistent with the scene, shallow depth of field.

overall_soundscape: {环境音 1～4 句}

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

- 第一行对齐句逐字照抄（官方固定），后面空一行。
- 不写负面提示词（H3 无负面分支）；要什么就正面写，例如「exactly two people in the frame」。
- 全英文；画面里要出现的中文字原样放英文双引号里。

## 2. 运镜句（类型 + 幅度 + 速度）

| 白模机位 | 句式 |
|---|---|
| 固定 | `The camera holds a static shot` |
| 推近 | `The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward {对象}` |
| 拉远 | `The camera pulls out with small amplitude at slow speed, revealing {新露出的东西}` |
| 环绕 | `The camera moves in an arc shot around {对象} with small amplitude at slow speed` |
| 跟拍 | `A tracking shot follows {对象} at walking pace, keeping the framing steady` |
| 手持 | `The handheld camera shakes slightly, staying close on {对象}` |
| 横移 / 摇 / 升降 | `The camera trucks left/right ...`、`pans left/right ...`、`tilts up/down ...`、`pedestals up/down ...` |

幅度：with small / large amplitude；速度：at slow / fast speed；中等幅度、正常速度不写。

## 3. 说话

```text
{年龄 性别 音高 音色 语速，如 a calm 21-year-old young man with a clear, steady mid-pitched voice} (S1) says with realistic lip articulation and no exaggerated mouth opening: <d>[Chinese] {原话，标点照抄}</d>
```

- 说话人第一次出现写全声音特征，之后用 the same speaker / he / she；编号全片固定。
- 一句话一个 `<d>`；约 15 秒不超过 20 个词，长台词拆镜头。
- 画外音：`(S1) says in an off-screen voiceover: <d>...</d> while his lips remain completely closed.`
- 不说话的镜头：`Nobody speaks in this shot; lips stay relaxed and closed except for natural breathing.`
- 后期另配音、再用口型同步模型对嘴时，可只写 `At about {t} seconds {说话人} (S1) starts speaking naturally, lips moving with realistic articulation, for about {n} seconds.`

## 4. 常见地点的环境音（overall_soundscape）

| 地点 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 宿舍白天 | Quiet dormitory room tone with a faint computer fan and occasional distant footsteps in the corridor. Fabric rustles softly as people move, and chairs creak lightly. |
| 宿舍夜里 | A quiet dormitory at night: low room tone, the faint hum of a desk lamp, distant muffled voices from the corridor, and soft breathing. |
| 图书馆 | The hushed room tone of a large library, pages turning nearby, a pen scratching on paper and the soft scrape of a chair on the floor. |
| 食堂 | A busy university canteen: overlapping distant chatter, trays and chopsticks clinking, food being served at the counter. |
| 小教室 / 会议室 | A small meeting room with faint ventilation hum, chairs shifting on the floor and the low murmur of a few students. |
| 户外白天 | An open campus in daylight with light wind through the trees, birds, distant footsteps and bicycles passing. |
| 户外夜里 | A campus sports field at night with a light breeze, distant traffic, faint footsteps on the running track and insects chirping. |
| 家里客厅 | A quiet apartment living room with a ticking clock and faint street noise outside the window. |
| 走廊 | A tiled corridor with echoing footsteps and a distant door closing. |

## 5. 首帧（白模 → 写实照片，中文前缀）

```text
画面中的人物是{每人：性别 年龄 体型（发型，穿{完整衣着}[，眼镜]）}、…，不要改变人物的性别、人数和衣着。{kf_hint}
```

kf_hint（多人或易错镜头才写）：
```text
从左到右：左边是{谁，体型，衣着}，中间是{…}，右边是{…}；画面里只有这 {N} 个人。
```

## 6. 人物外貌怎么写（中英文都要）

- 顺序：年龄性别 → **体型的整体轮廓**（偏胖/清瘦/高挑，不点名身体部位）→ 脸型五官 → 发型发色 → **完整衣着**（外套、内搭、裤子，近景看不到的帽子也写上）→ 眼镜等配饰。
- 中英文、白模服装、负面词三处一致；剧本里的外貌描述也要和人物库逐字一致。
- 例：`a noticeably overweight, chubby 20-year-old Chinese male college student with a broad heavy stocky build, fully clothed, round face, small eyes, short messy black hair, dark grey open zip hoodie with the hood resting behind his neck over a dark grey T-shirt, dark blue jeans`

## 7. 情绪和细节的安全写法

| 想表达 | 别写 | 写 |
|---|---|---|
| 哭过 / 感动 | eyes red, eyes turning red | eyes slightly puffy from crying, eyes growing moist, natural eyes |
| 胖 | round belly, big stomach | broad heavy stocky build, fully clothed |
| 镜头里只露一部分的人 | 只写这个人 | 照白模写清楚露出的是什么（only his shoulder is visible at the left edge） |
| 低头 / 只露头顶 | face clearly visible | at first only the top of his hair is visible, he slowly lifts his head |
| 人数 | no extra people | exactly {N} people in the frame |
