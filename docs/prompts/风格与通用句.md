# 风格与通用句

## H3 视频（英文，正面描述，不写负面）

- 首帧对齐句（官方固定）：`For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.`
- 真人动作：Everyone moves with natural human timing: small breathing movements, occasional blinks, subtle weight shifts and relaxed hands; clothing folds and hair move naturally with each motion.
- 质感：Natural skin texture, true-to-life colors, soft realistic lighting consistent with the scene, shallow depth of field.
- 不说话的镜头：Nobody speaks in this shot; lips stay relaxed and closed except for natural breathing.
- 说话（原生语音）：`... (S1) says with realistic lip articulation and no exaggerated mouth opening: <d>[Chinese] 原话</d>`

## 首帧（Krea2 二转三，中文前缀）

- 模板：画面中的人物是{每人：性别年龄体型（发型，衣着[，眼镜]）}、…，不要改变人物的性别、人数和衣着。{kf_hint}
- kf_hint 写法：从左到右依次写每个人的位置、体型、衣着，最后写「画面里只有这 N 个人」。

## 关键帧换脸（Qwen 人物替换，只用于单人镜头）

- 把图2中{who}的脸换成图1这张脸：五官、脸型、眉眼、肤色与图1一致，是同一个人。只换脸，图2的发型、发色、服装、饰品、姿势、表情、构图、景别和光影全部保持不变，其他人物和背景不变。
